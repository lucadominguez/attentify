3. What the software does to your computer

This matters more than in most software, so we are being explicit. To enforce blocking, the desktop app runs with administrator rights and may:

  • edit your hosts file to redirect blocked domains
  • add firewall rules
  • close running applications you have chosen to block
  • change browser policy settings
  • stop programs from launching at startup, when you ask it to
  • read your browser history, when you ask it to import

The app keeps a change journal and supports system restore points so changes can be undone, and uninstalling is designed to clean up after itself. Even so, you are responsible for deciding whether to grant these permissions on a machine you control and are allowed to modify.
